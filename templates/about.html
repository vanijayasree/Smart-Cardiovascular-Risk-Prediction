💓 About Heart Disease Prediction System

This system leverages Machine Learning to analyze patient health data, predict the risk of heart disease, and provide actionable recommendations. It helps both doctors and patients in early diagnosis, prevention, and decision-making.

❤️ What is Heart Disease?

Heart disease refers to several types of conditions that affect the heart, including coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, heart failure, and congenital defects. It is one of the leading causes of death worldwide, making early detection essential.

⚠️ Why It Occurs?

🧬 Biological & Medical Factors

High blood pressure, high cholesterol, diabetes, obesity, and family history contribute to heart disease risk.

🚬 Lifestyle Factors

Smoking, alcohol use, unhealthy diet, lack of exercise, and chronic stress increase the chances of heart disease.

📌 Major Risk Factors

💉 Hypertension

Uncontrolled high blood pressure damages arteries and strains the heart.

🍔 Poor Diet

High intake of trans fats, salt, and sugar leads to obesity and cardiovascular issues.

🛑 Lack of Exercise

Inactive lifestyle increases the risk of obesity, diabetes, and hypertension.

🩺 Prevention & Care

🥦 Healthy Diet

Eat more vegetables, fruits, and whole grains for a healthy heart.

🏃 Regular Exercise

Engage in at least 30 minutes of physical activity daily.

🚭 No Smoking

Avoid smoking and limit alcohol consumption to protect your heart.

🧘 Stress Management

Practice yoga, meditation, or relaxation techniques to reduce stress.
