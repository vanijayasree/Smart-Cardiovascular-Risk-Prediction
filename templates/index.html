✨ Features

🔍 Prediction

Analyzes user’s health inputs like age, blood pressure, cholesterol, and lifestyle factors to predict the likelihood of heart disease using trained ML models.

👤 Authentication

Provides secure user registration and login with password encryption, ensuring data privacy and restricted access to sensitive health information.

📊 Visualization

Displays prediction results in graphs and dashboards, making it easier for users to understand risk levels and follow recommendations.

Step 1

User registers or logs in securely to access the system.

Step 2

Health details such as age, BP, cholesterol, and lifestyle inputs are provided.

Step 3

ML model processes the data, evaluates heart disease probability, and identifies potential risk levels.

Step 4

Prediction and personalized recommendations are shown with a detailed report.
